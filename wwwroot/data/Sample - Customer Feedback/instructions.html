Sample Project: Customer Feedback

This is a demo corpus showing the classic suggestion workflow, where the tool is set up for straightforward named-entity annotation over short pieces of free text.

What to annotate

  • Person — names of individual people
  • Organization — companies, teams, or departments
  • Location — cities, offices, or other places
  • Product — named products or features
  • Date — dates or date-like phrases

Try it out

  • Records fb-001 and fb-004 already have annotations — use Next Annotated to jump straight to them.
  • Records fb-003, fb-007, fb-009, and fb-012 have AI suggestions. They show up with a red border and a "?" suffix (e.g. "Person?") — click one to accept it as a real annotation.
  • Pick a span type button, then select text anywhere in the record to create a new annotation from scratch.

This corpus is a good starting point if you want a config with no extra bells and whistles: no context panel, no external link, no annotation confinement.
